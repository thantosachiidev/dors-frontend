About Mina

Attention is a form
of care.

I am a Nigerian photographer working between commissioned and personal practice. I make images for people who care about the texture of a story: independent publications, cultural spaces, and brands with something real to say.

My process is unhurried, collaborative, and grounded in looking closely. Recent clients include The Gentlewoman, Apartamento, and Studio Nari.
